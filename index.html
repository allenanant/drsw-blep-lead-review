Sales summary

  • There are 283 leads in this report. The focus is who the team actually spoke to and what concerns those people raised.
  • 255 leads (90.1%) said the price on the form was within their budget. Of these, 169 wanted treatment within three months (59.7% of all leads).
  • The checked calls show that the team spoke to at least 109 leads. This includes calls made by the team and by leads. We count only conversations supported by the records.
  • At least 73 leads discussed treatment, prices or other details of their enquiry with the team. The other 36 spoken-to leads had only a brief chat or discussed practical arrangements.
  • 56 leads told the team they were busy. This is different from a call getting a busy tone.
  • 50 leads said the price was within their budget and wanted treatment within three months, but had no call attempt recorded.

How the review was checked
  • 23 September recheck: the 44 unclear recordings were transcribed again, and all four missing-audio call records were checked in GHL. The new Jev review is not complete: OpenRouter rejected the saved key with HTTP 401. No new Jev results are included.

Coverage and method

  • Model: Saved typesafe/jev-1.13 classifications plus Codex transcript review
  • Transcription: Saved Gemini transcripts plus local faster-whisper-medium; no further paid transcription
  • No call is treated as a conversation based on duration alone.
  • No recording available means GHL did not provide audio for that event. It does not, by itself, mean the lead answered or did not answer.
  • We use GHL call records to identify unanswered calls, busy lines and failed calls, unless a recording shows something different.
  • If the records do not tell us what happened, we mark the call as unknown.
  • We check the AI results before using them in the totals.
  • Cutoff: 20 September 2026, 23:59 London. Calls from 21 September onward excluded.
  • Saved Jev classifications cover 588 call records. Codex reviewed 179 additional call records after OpenRouter credits ran out; these are not Jev results.
  • Existing Gemini transcripts retained. Remaining recordings transcribed with free local Whisper, one at a time.
  • Conclusions use automated transcripts and GHL call status, not a claim that a human listened to every recording.
  • Automated transcription/classification can make mistakes; ambiguous evidence remains unknown.
  • Long means a recording of at least 120 seconds with a substantive exchange; it is not exact talk time.
  • No duration-only voicemail or human-conversation labels.
  • Call records without audio use GHL technical status; completed status without audio remains unknown.
  • Unique frozen call IDs matched to lead and date.
  • Outgoing and incoming totals kept separate.
  • Independent transcript QA for disputed cases; original model results retained.
